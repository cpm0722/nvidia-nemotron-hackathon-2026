Validation rules for arca.live posts:
- The post title or body must directly discuss the queried AI product — not just mention it incidentally (e.g., comparing many models in passing).
- Meaningful content: the post must contain actual user opinion, experience, benchmark result, or factual claim about the product. Memes, off-topic chat, or single-line posts with no substance should be removed.
- Language: Korean posts are expected and valid. Do not remove items solely because they are in Korean.
- Comments count: posts with at least one comment are generally more informative, but a post with zero comments can still be valid if the body is substantive.
- Remove items where the body is empty or contains only image/link placeholders with no text.
