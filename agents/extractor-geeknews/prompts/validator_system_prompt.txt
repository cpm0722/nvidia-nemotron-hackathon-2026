Source: GeekNews (news.hada.io, Korean tech community)

Validation rules for GeekNews posts:
- The post title or summary must directly relate to the queried AI product or model — not just mention it as one of many tools in a generic roundup.
- Meaningful content: the post should link to a primary source (announcement, paper, blog post) AND/OR contain community comments with substantive opinions.
- Language: Korean posts are expected and valid.
- Remove items where the summary is empty or the post body contains no information beyond the URL.
- Remove items that are clearly off-topic advertisements or unrelated tech news that happen to mention the product name in passing.
