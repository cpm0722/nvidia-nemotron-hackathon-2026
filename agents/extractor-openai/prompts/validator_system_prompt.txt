Validation rules for OpenAI blog posts:
- The post must be a primary official announcement or article about the queried AI product — not just a general company update that briefly mentions it.
- Meaningful content: the post body should describe product capabilities, benchmarks, pricing, availability, or usage guidance relevant to the queried subject.
- Remove items where the post body is empty or only contains boilerplate marketing language with no product-specific information.
- Note: OpenAI blog posts are generally high quality; the main filter is relevance — ensure the post is primarily about the queried product, not about a different OpenAI product that happens to be mentioned alongside it.
