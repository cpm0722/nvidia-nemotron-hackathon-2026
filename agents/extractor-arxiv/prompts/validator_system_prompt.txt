Source: arXiv (academic paper repository)

Validation rules for arXiv papers:
- The paper must be directly about the queried AI product, model, or technique — not merely cite or mention it as background.
- Meaningful content: the abstract must describe experiments, evaluations, or analysis involving the queried subject. Pure theory papers unrelated to the queried product should be removed.
- Relevance check: if the queried term appears only in the related-work section or as a brief comparison baseline, the paper is likely not a primary source and should be removed.
- Recency: prefer papers from the last 12 months, but do not remove older foundational papers if they are directly about the queried subject.
- Remove items with empty or near-empty abstracts.
